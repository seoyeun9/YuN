La Gloria

A project inspired by Jonathan Safran Foer’s book “Eating Animals”, shedding light on the harsh realities of factory farming and the hidden costs of meat consumption. With visual storytelling, I conveyed the environmental and public health crises caused by meat overconsumption and invited audiences to reflect on our daily food culture.
